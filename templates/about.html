About Our Project

Meet ScamShield AI

A machine learning-based project designed to help job seekers recognize potential fraud indicators in online job advertisements and make more informed decisions.

🎯 Our Mission

Online job advertisements can sometimes contain misleading claims, suspicious payment requests, unrealistic salary promises, or attempts to collect sensitive personal information. ScamShield AI aims to help users examine these warning signs through automated text analysis and risk classification.

💡 What Does ScamShield AI Do?

The application uses a trained machine learning model to analyze job advertisement text. It provides a prediction, an estimated fraud probability, a risk category, and potentially suspicious phrases or warning signals. The results are intended to support—not replace—independent verification.

🤖

Machine Learning

A trained text-classification model evaluates job advertisement content and produces a prediction based on patterns learned during training.

🚩

Warning Detection

Additional checks highlight potential red flags such as upfront fees, guaranteed selection, urgent pressure, and requests for sensitive information.

📊

Dashboard Insights

The dashboard summarizes recorded checks, risk classifications, and recent analyses stored by the application.

🧭

Practical Guidance

The result page offers a risk assessment and a recommendation to help users decide what to verify before trusting a job offer.

⚙️ How It Works

Follow these simple steps to analyze a job advertisement.

1

Enter Job Details

Paste the text of the job advertisement into the analysis form.

2

Analyze the Text

The model and warning checks examine the provided text for potential scam indicators.

3

Review the Result

Read the predicted classification, risk level, detected warnings, and recommended precautions.

🛠️ Technology Stack

Backend: Python and Flask
Machine Learning: Trained text classification model
Database: SQLite
Frontend: HTML and CSS
Integration: JSON API for the browser extension

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

ScamShield AI is a decision-support project, not a guarantee that a job advertisement is genuine or fraudulent. Predictions and risk scores may be incorrect. Always verify the employer through official channels, never pay recruitment fees without careful verification, and avoid sharing sensitive information with unverified recruiters.
